Who we're supporting
Covenant House gives young people facing homelessness a safe place to sleep, and a path to what comes next.

Since 1972, Covenant House has opened its doors to more than 1.5 million young people — offering shelter, meals, healthcare, counseling, education and job training across 34 cities in five countries. Their doors are open every hour of every day.

Why we sleep out
"
One night can't replicate homelessness.
It can help change what comes next.

We are not pretending to understand. We are choosing to pay attention, and to put real money behind it. For one night we trade a bed for a sleeping bag and a hard surface, and we spend the hours before dawn learning from the people who do this work.

How it goes.
Evening
Arrive and check in
Meet the Accenture group, find your spot on the field, settle in.
Before lights out
Hear from the people doing the work
We hear from Covenant House leaders and from people who have been through the program themselves. Their stories are the part of the night participants remember most.
Overnight
Sleep out
Outside, in a sleeping bag, on a hard surface, alongside your colleagues.
The next day
Carry it into your day
An early finish, then you carry your full day of work and responsibilities on very little sleep — a small glimpse of what some young people face without the support Covenant House provides.
